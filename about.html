About Dodee & Onur

Dodee & Onur is a warm YouTube Kids series for ages 0–4. Through songs, gentle stories, and playful moments, little ones can enjoy English in a calm, parent-friendly way — free on YouTube.

Episode 1 lands 23 April 2026. Until then, join the fun on the home page: countdown, daily colour game, mystery clues, and a launch alert so you don’t miss the first episode.
