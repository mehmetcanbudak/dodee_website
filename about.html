About Dodee & Onur

Dodee & Onur is a warm YouTube Kids series for ages 0–4. Through songs, gentle stories, and playful moments, little ones can enjoy English in a calm, parent-friendly way — free on YouTube. Episode 1 lands 23 April 2026.

✨ Meet the Stars

The Heart of Dodee’s World

A fluffy green buddy and a passionate educator — together, they’re making English the most joyful language a little one can learn.

🟢

Dodee

Your Fluffy Green Buddy

Dodee is a gender-neutral, endlessly curious little creature who loves learning — even when they make mistakes! With a bright orange polka-dot bandana and a golden star badge, Dodee makes every English word feel like a magical discovery. Children love helping Dodee when things go wrong — because learning from mistakes is the best kind of learning.

Onur Seyhoğlu

Educator · Musician · Ventriloquist

Onur brings warmth, energy, and genuine educational expertise to every episode. As a musician and ventriloquist, he creates a uniquely engaging live performance that young children can’t look away from. His carefully crafted interactions with Dodee follow evidence-based language acquisition principles — every word, pause, and song is intentional.
