Privacy Policy

This page describes how the Dodee & Onur promotion website (the “Site”) handles personal information. It applies only to this promotional site, not to YouTube, social platforms, or third-party services you may reach through our links.

Email and contact

Email signup and contact forms are not available on this site. The site does not collect newsletter registrations or direct inquiries.

Website operation

The site’s own code does not include an analytics service or set tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the site.

Browser storage and games

The color game stores your streak and the date of your last correct answer in your browser’s local storage. The site does not send those values to a server. You can remove them by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Fonts, videos, and external links

Fonts and the teaser preview are hosted on this site. The teaser loads YouTube’s privacy-enhanced video player only when you select Play. This connects your browser to YouTube, which may receive technical information such as your IP address. The video provider may process additional information when you interact with or play a video. Following a YouTube link also opens that external service.

External platforms have their own privacy policies. Read Google’s privacy policy for information about YouTube.

Parents and caregivers

This promotional site is intended for parents and caregivers. It does not provide account registration or forms requesting a child’s personal information.

Contact

Direct inquiry contact details are not published yet. Current availability is listed on the Contact page.
