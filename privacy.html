Privacy Policy

This page describes how the Dodee & Onur promotional website (“we”, “us”) handles information. Last updated: April 2026.

What we collect

The launch signup form on this site is currently a demonstration and does not send your email to our servers. When a real email provider is connected, we will update this policy to explain what is collected and why.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may process technical data such as IP address, browser type, and approximate location for security and reliability.

Cookies and local storage

Interactive features (for example, the color game streak) may store small amounts of data in your browser using localStorage. This stays on your device and is not sent to us by default.

Children’s privacy

This site is intended for parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have received information from a child, please contact us so we can delete it.
